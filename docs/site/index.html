Frequently asked questions

Why do fuel dispensers need to be inspected?

Fuel sold by volume is regulated under Canada's Weights and Measures Act. Measurement Canada requires dispensers to be inspected at set intervals so customers get exactly what they pay for — and so stations aren't giving fuel away either.

Are you authorized to certify dispensers today?

Not yet. We've completed the Measurement Canada theory exam and are working toward the practical evaluation, targeted for April 2027. Until then, we're happy to talk about your sites and add you to our early client list so you're first in line.

What test equipment will you use?

Only provers and test measures on Measurement Canada's recognized equipment list, verified as local standards — including vapour-retention measures for gasoline.

Who do you work with?

Independent gas stations, marinas, bulk plants, farms and fleet operators with on-site fuel — anyone in our area with equipment that measures fuel for sale.
