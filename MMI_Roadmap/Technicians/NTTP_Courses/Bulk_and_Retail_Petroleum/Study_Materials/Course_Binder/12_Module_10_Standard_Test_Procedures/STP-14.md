## Performance — Backlash test

### Application
This test is performed on dispensers and refuellers only.

### Purpose
Quick repetitive starting and stopping of product flow is a normal procedure in topping up a vehicle's fuel tank. This test determines if excessive backlash in the system will affect the delivered volume of product.

**Legislative references:** SVM.1-35, SVM.1-20, R.265.

### Procedure
Run a fast flow test and determine the accuracy of the meter.

Repeat the fast flow test, stopping and starting the product flow quickly, approximately five times.

### Interpretation of results
The second delivery must not be outside the limits of error.
