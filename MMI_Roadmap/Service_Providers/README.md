## OPCA / CPCA Overlap — The Field Hours Angle

Most of these firms are also members of the **Ontario Petroleum Contractors Association** or **CPCA** — the same associations that run the PM.1 course. OPCA members frequently take on PMH-certified trainees.

Confirmed OPCA / CPCA member directory listings: Tanknology (Englobe), National Energy Equipment, Cantest Solutions, P.D. McLaren, Albert's Gas Station Maintenance, Wagg's Petroleum Equipment.

**Best field-hours prospects from this list** (working outward from Wasaga Beach):

1. **Wagg's Petroleum Equipment** — North Bay. Installs and services everything, longest-established, and covers the underserved north. A trainee who can also cover Simcoe is useful to them.
2. **Albert's Gas Station Maintenance** — Cambridge. Small enough to talk to the owner directly; does exactly the dispenser work PM.1 covers.
3. **National Energy Equipment** — Mississauga. Largest service department on the list, most likely to have a formal trainee slot.
4. **Cantest Solutions** — Toronto. Pure certification work — closest to MMI's Measurement Canada side, though lighter on the wrench work PM.1 hours require.

> These are additions to — not replacements for — the closer leads already tracked in
> [Petroleum Equipment Mechanic → The 1,000 Hours](../Technicians/TSSA_Courses/Petroleum_Equipment_Mechanic/README.md#the-1000-hours--finding-a-licensed-pm-near-wasaga-beach).
> **Comco Canada (Barrie, ~30 min) and OPCA head office (Barrie) remain the first calls** on proximity alone.

> "I hold Measurement Canada NTTP theoretical certification for Bulk and Retail Petroleum, and I'm working toward my TSSA PMH and PM.1. I'm based in Wasaga Beach. I'm looking to log field hours under a licensed PM — and I'm local to the Simcoe / Georgian Bay sites you're currently driving to. Is that something your company would use?"
